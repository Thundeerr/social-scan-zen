# Discovery — North Star Doctrine

InstaScanner Discovery is NOT an Instagram browser. It is an **AI-powered Luxury Signal Extraction Engine**. It continuously transforms millions of weak signals into a handful of exceptional opportunities. Quality > quantity, always. Every click must carry extremely high informational value.

Every future Discovery feature is evaluated against ONE question:

> "Does this increase the quality of discoveries, or does it simply increase the amount of data?"

If the answer is "more data" — cut it.

## The Pipeline

Every stage MUST reduce noise and increase signal quality. Nothing bypasses stages.

```
Real World Place
   ↓
Resolve Place                (Nominatim / Google Places / OSM)
   ↓
Resolve Instagram Locations  (contextual name+city+country match)
   ↓
Collect Signals              (posts, tags, co-appearances, captions)
   ↓
Discover Accounts
   ↓
Discover Posts
   ↓
Quality Gate                 (aggressive rejection of mediocrity)
   ↓
AI Understanding             (per-axis scoring + reasons)
   ↓
Relationship Graph           (provenance, co-appearance, clusters)
   ↓
Ranking                      (base + learning + novelty − diversity, entropy floor)
   ↓
Operator Inbox
```

## The Quality Gate

The Quality Gate exists to **reject mediocrity**, not to detect content. If it appears in the Inbox, the operator must be able to trust it is worth attention.

Aggressive rejection targets:
- spam, repost spam, meme pages
- low-quality media, watermarked content
- generic tourist photos
- repetitive creators, duplicate/near-duplicate media
- near-identical friend groups (collapse via clusters)
- obvious engagement bait
- accounts the operator has repeatedly ignored
- low-value clusters

Rule: the operator should rarely see bad content. Every gate rejection is preferable to one mediocre card in the Inbox.

## Hidden Gems

Discovery optimizes for hidden gems — not follower count.

Examples of valuable, non-mainstream signals:
- photographers working with Aman / Rosewood / Soneva
- hotel videographers
- private pilots, yacht crew, yacht owners
- architects, luxury real estate creators
- travel couples, private individuals living exceptional lifestyles

Actively surface people **before** they become mainstream creators. Follower count is a weak signal; provenance, co-appearance density with tracked accounts, and location patterns are strong signals.

## Relationship Intelligence

Never treat an account as an isolated object. The graph itself is one of the platform's most valuable assets.

Every candidate must answer:
- Who introduced this account? (provenance chain)
- Who appears with them?
- Which luxury places connect them?
- Which creators repeatedly interact?
- Which communities/clusters exist?

Design every new feature to strengthen the graph (edges, provenance, clusters, novelty distance), not just add nodes.

## Explainability

The operator must never blindly trust the AI. Every recommendation answers:
- Why was this discovered? (provenance chain)
- Why is it valuable? (concrete signal lines)
- What evidence supports this? (captions, tags, locations)
- What signals increased confidence?
- What signals reduced confidence?

An AI score without reasons attached is a bug. Hide the score rather than show it bare.

## Continuous Learning

Every operator decision — Track / Ignore / Blacklist — is training data. It continuously improves:
- ranking
- discovery
- diversity
- novelty
- entropy control

Two operators sharing seeds must eventually receive **different Inboxes**. If Inboxes stay identical, the learning loop is broken.

## Operator Experience

The operator should never feel like they are searching. They should feel the system already searched for them.

Every Discovery session should end with:

> "I found something I never would have found manually."

## Future Vision

Discovery must eventually move beyond Instagram locations and understand real-world luxury ecosystems:

```
Aman Tokyo → Photographers → Guests → Architects → Luxury Creators
           → Private Individuals → Hotels → Yachts → Restaurants
           → Events → new hidden networks
```

The objective is not to monitor Instagram. The objective is to **continuously discover elite content sources before everyone else.**

## Feature test (must pass ALL)

1. Does it increase discovery **quality**, not just data volume?
2. Does every stage in the pipeline still reduce noise?
3. Does the Quality Gate reject more mediocrity, or let more through?
4. Does it favor hidden gems over popular accounts?
5. Does it strengthen the relationship graph (provenance, clusters, novelty)?
6. Is every score/recommendation explainable with concrete reasons?
7. Does operator decision data feed back into ranking?
8. Would the operator end the session saying "I found something I never would have found manually"?

If any answer is "no" — rework or cut.
